## 📱 The 9 Core Modules

1. **Module 1 — Trip & Budget Planner (The Financial Spine)**:
   - 5 Auto-balanced category buckets: Stay (35%), Travel (20%), Food (20%), Activities (15%), Buffer/Emergency (10%).
   - **Multi-Currency Engine**: Instant conversion between INR, USD, EUR, and GBP.
   - Safe daily allowance calculator and live burn rate gauge.
2. **Module 2 — Stay & Travel Aggregator (with Eco-Certification)**:
   - Eco-friendly lodging filter (solar-powered, zero single-use plastics) & electric transit (EV tuk-tuks, Jaipur Metro).
   - **Live Weather Advisory**: Warns of extreme afternoon heat or rain, recommending air-cooled indoor cultural sanctuaries.
   - One-click *Reserve & Deduct* decrements the live Stay or Travel bucket balance.
3. **Module 3 — Local Culture & Hidden Heritage Layer**:
   - Curated authentic dataset for Jaipur: Panna Meena ka Kund, Anokhi Museum of Hand Printing, Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan, LMB Ghewar.
   - Interactive Leaflet OpenStreetMap with crowd hotspot indicators.
4. **Module 4 — Emergency & Safety Hub (Offline PWA)**:
   - One-tap SOS beacon packaging live GPS coordinates (`26.9855° N, 75.8513° E`) into an emergency SMS to 112.
   - Service Worker caches 24/7 Level-1 trauma centers (SMS Govt Hospital) and Tourist Police lines.
5. **Module 5 — Multilingual Context-Aware AI Chatbot (AI Sahayak)**:
   - Hydrated with current destination, booked hostel, and exact remaining budget balance.
   - Supports **English, Hindi (हिंदी), Tamil (தமிழ்), and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)**.
6. **Module 6 — Group Split (Splitwise Engine)**:
   - Greedy debt minimization algorithm simplifying multi-party bills down to minimal direct payments.
   - Dynamic UPI Settlement QR generator.
7. **Module 7 — GPS Proactive Crowd Alert & Over-Tourism Rerouting**:
   - Simulates GPS proximity to high-density monuments (e.g. Amer Fort peak hours); pushes low-crowd alternative hidden gems (`hidden_gem: true`).
   - Direct answer to jury over-tourism and sustainability questions.
8. **Module 8 — Hyper-Local Regional Language Audio Guide & Inscription Plaque Scanner**:
   - Working epigraphy scanner: matches photographed ancient stone plaques (Amer Fort 1612 AD, Sarnath Ashoka Pillar 250 BC, Belur Hoysala 1117 AD).
   - Speaks translated historical context aloud using Web Speech API TTS in EN, HI, TA, KN.
   - Live speech-to-speech tour guide translator simulator.
9. **Module 9 — Role-Based Auth & Government Tourism Department Dashboard**:
   - Seamless role switcher between **Tourist Mode** and **Government Admin Mode**.
   - Live tourist footfall capacity meters by monument.
   - Congestion diversion metrics (+38% redirected to hidden gems).
   - Emergency SOS dispatch incident monitor.
   - Regional tourist language demand statistics.
